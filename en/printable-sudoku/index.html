Free printable sudoku

Free printable sudoku, one per page: pick a level, open the puzzle and download the A4 PDF. The answers are always on page 2, so you can hand the sheet to someone else without spoiling it.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, checked by the program that generated it: you solve them with logic, no guessing. Each page also shows the grid on screen, the rules and a hint on where to start.

Besides classic sudoku there are two variants: X-Sudoku, with the diagonals, and jigsaw sudoku, with irregular regions. If you prefer big digits, the large print puzzles are easy to read.

No sign-up, no ads. The site is made by the developer of Grafite Sudoku, the Android app: if one day you want to play on your phone too, you will find it on Google Play.

Easy printable sudoku

These easy sudoku puzzles need no advanced techniques. At every step there is a cell where only one number fits, or a number with only one free spot in a row, column or box. Those are "singles", and they are all you need.

Medium printable sudoku

In medium sudoku, singles are no longer enough: at some point the grid seems stuck and you need one more idea. Usually it is a hidden pair or a locked candidate, techniques you can learn in minutes and that change how you read a puzzle.

Hard printable sudoku

Printable X-Sudoku (diagonal sudoku)

X-Sudoku, also called diagonal sudoku, has the classic rules plus one: both long diagonals of the grid must also contain every number from 1 to 9, with no repeats. The diagonals are shaded, so you can see them at a glance.

  • X-Sudoku #1easy · 32 clues
  • X-Sudoku #2easy · 32 clues
  • X-Sudoku #3medium · 30 clues
  • X-Sudoku #4medium · 29 clues

Printable jigsaw sudoku

In jigsaw sudoku (also called irregular sudoku) there are no 3×3 boxes: the grid is split into nine free-form regions of nine cells each. Rows and columns work as usual, and each region must contain the numbers 1 to 9.

  • Jigsaw Sudoku #1easy · 32 clues
  • Jigsaw Sudoku #2easy · 32 clues
  • Jigsaw Sudoku #3medium · 28 clues
