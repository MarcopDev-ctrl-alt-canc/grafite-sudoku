The skyscrapers puzzle (City)

The skyscrapers puzzle is a cousin of sudoku. In Grafite Sudoku it is called City, and it is a mode you only find in the app.

The rules

  • Every cell is a building, with a height from 1 up to the size of the grid.
  • Each height appears exactly once in every row and every column.
  • The numbers on the edge say how many buildings you see from there along that row or column: a lower building is hidden behind a taller one.

First strategies

  • A clue equal to the grid size means buildings in increasing order: 1, 2, 3, 4.
  • A clue of 1 means the tallest building is right in front of you.
  • A high clue keeps tall buildings out of the first cells: they would hide the ones behind.

In the City of Grafite Sudoku the grids are 5×5 and 6×6, with dedicated lessons in the Sudoku Academy.

In the app: "What you can see" lesson in the Sudoku Academy

In the Sudoku Academy of Grafite Sudoku this technique is an interactive lesson on the grid, with exercises from real puzzles.
