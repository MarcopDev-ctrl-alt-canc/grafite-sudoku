Why Grafite Sudoku

A sudoku made to be as nice to look at as it is to play, and to respect the people who play it.

🚫

No ads, no account

No banners, no forced videos, no sign-up. The app is supported by a coffee from whoever wants to offer one.

🧩

Five ways to play

Classic, X-Sudoku with the diagonals, Jigsaw with irregular regions, 12×12 and City, the skyscrapers puzzle.

🎓

Sudoku Academy

Dozens of lessons on the grid, exercises from real puzzles and a final exam. In a game, the "Which technique?" help tells you what to look for without giving away the number.

🎨

Paper themes

Several paper textures for the grid, from cream to chalkboard, so you play on a page that feels real.

📈

Levels, records and trophies

Every game earns experience. The game celebrates your personal bests, with dozens of trophies and avatars to unlock.

⏱️

Daily Challenge and Time Attack

A new puzzle every day, with your day streak, and timed games at rising difficulty.

🤝

Duel your friends

Send your game's code to a friend: same puzzle, times compared.

🌍

Seven languages

English, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, German and French.

Waiting for you in the full app

Every milestone unlocks a travel companion: nine avatar lines, each with several stages of growth.
